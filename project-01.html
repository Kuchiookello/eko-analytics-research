BUSINESS CONTEXT

The management challenge

Small and medium-sized enterprises frequently collect sales and customer information through invoices, spreadsheets, point-of-sale systems and basic record-keeping tools.

Yet having data does not automatically mean having useful management information.

Project 01 demonstrates how transaction-level records can be converted into a structured performance view covering revenue, customer behaviour, service mix, geographic performance and management priorities.

CORE MANAGEMENT QUESTIONS

What does the business need to understand?

  • How is revenue performing over time?
  • Which services generate the highest commercial value?
  • Which customer groups contribute most strongly?
  • Which locations generate stronger or weaker results?
  • Which acquisition channels are producing revenue?
  • What should management prioritise next?

KEY FINDINGS

What the analysis reveals

Business Advisory leads service revenue

Business Advisory emerged as the strongest service by revenue in the demonstration dataset.

Nairobi is the leading market

Nairobi generated the strongest geographic revenue contribution across the simulated markets.

Micro businesses lead customer revenue

The Micro Business segment produced the largest customer-segment revenue contribution.

Website is the strongest revenue channel

Website-originated activity produced the strongest revenue contribution among the simulated acquisition channels.

CUSTOMER PERFORMANCE

Retention and concentration matter

The demonstration dataset contains 80 unique customers. A substantial proportion recorded repeat activity, showing why SMEs should analyse customer retention alongside new customer acquisition.

The ten largest customers generated approximately 21.5% of total revenue. Customer concentration should therefore be monitored as part of commercial risk management.

MANAGEMENT RECOMMENDATIONS

Turning findings into action

Protect High-Value Services

Prioritise sales capacity and delivery quality around services that generate the strongest commercial contribution.

Build Repeat Business

Create structured follow-up, retention and repeat-purchase processes for existing customers.

Forecast Monthly Performance

Use monthly KPI reviews and sales-pipeline tracking to identify revenue gaps early.

Monitor Customer Concentration

Track dependence on major customers and deliberately broaden the active customer base.

Use Market-Specific Strategies

Compare customer demand, service mix and acquisition channels before allocating resources across locations.

Measure Channel Economics

Add marketing spend, lead volumes and conversion rates to assess channel return on investment.

PROJECT LIMITATIONS

Transparency matters

The numerical results in Project 01 are illustrative because the underlying transaction records were created specifically for demonstration.

The results should therefore not be interpreted as industry benchmarks, market statistics or evidence about a particular company.

A live engagement would additionally include

  • Client-specific data validation
  • Actual cost and profitability information
  • Marketing expenditure
  • Sales targets and budgets
  • Management interviews
  • Client-specific recommendations
