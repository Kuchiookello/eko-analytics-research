Customer Satisfaction & Service Quality Analysis

An evidence-based assessment of customer experience, satisfaction, service delivery performance and opportunities for improving customer retention and organisational service quality.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Understanding the Customer Experience

Customer satisfaction provides organisations with an important indication of whether their products, services and customer interactions are meeting expectations.

Project 02 demonstrates how EKO Analytics & Research can organise customer feedback into measurable indicators, identify service-quality gaps and translate customer experience data into practical management recommendations.

Project Profile

  • Project: Customer Satisfaction & Service Quality Analysis
  • Category: Customer Experience Analytics
  • Approach: Quantitative and qualitative analysis
  • Focus: Satisfaction, service quality and retention
  • Period: 2025–2026

The Challenge

Organisations frequently collect customer feedback but struggle to convert individual responses into clear evidence about service performance.

Management therefore needs structured analysis showing what customers value, where dissatisfaction occurs and which improvements should receive priority.

Project Objectives

  • Measure overall customer satisfaction.
  • Assess major dimensions of service quality.
  • Identify recurring customer pain points.
  • Examine customer loyalty and retention indicators.
  • Determine priority areas for service improvement.
  • Develop actionable management recommendations.
ANALYTICAL APPROACH

Methodology

The project uses a structured customer-experience analytical framework combining data preparation, descriptive analysis, satisfaction indicators, service-quality measures and interpretation of customer feedback.

Data Preparation

Customer responses are organised, validated and prepared for systematic analysis.

Satisfaction Analysis

Satisfaction levels and customer experience indicators are analysed across relevant service dimensions.

Performance Interpretation

Analytical findings are translated into clear priorities for customer service improvement.

KEY ANALYTICAL AREAS

What the Analysis Examines

Customer Indicators

  • Overall satisfaction
  • Service responsiveness
  • Reliability
  • Communication quality
  • Customer support
  • Likelihood of repeat engagement

Management Questions

  • Which areas perform strongly?
  • Where are customer expectations not being met?
  • Which service problems require priority action?
  • What factors influence satisfaction?
  • How can customer retention be strengthened?
RECOMMENDATIONS

From Customer Feedback to Management Action

The final stage of the project translates analytical evidence into practical recommendations. These may include service-process improvements, stronger customer communication, response-time monitoring, complaint-management systems and regular customer satisfaction tracking.
