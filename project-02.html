PROJECT 02 • CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE

Customer Satisfaction & Service Quality Analysis

A customer-experience research case study examining satisfaction, service quality, recommendation likelihood, repeat intention, complaints and opportunities for service improvement.

CASE STUDY OVERVIEW

Understanding what drives customer experience.

Project 02 demonstrates how customer survey data can be transformed into practical evidence for improving service quality, customer satisfaction and retention.

The project examines overall satisfaction alongside specific dimensions of service quality and customer behaviour.


ANALYTICAL QUESTIONS

What the project investigates

  • How satisfied are customers overall?
  • Which service-quality dimensions perform best?
  • Where are the largest customer-experience gaps?
  • How likely are customers to recommend the organisation?
  • What influences repeat-purchase or repeat-service intention?
  • Which customer segments require management attention?
