FIRST DISCUSSION

What happens after you contact EKO?

The first discussion is intended to understand your needs before recommending a scope of work.

01 — Understand the need

Clarify the business, research, programme or decision-making question.

02 — Review available information

Determine what data, documents, records or research already exist.

03 — Define the analytical approach

Identify the research or analytical method appropriate to the assignment.

04 — Agree deliverables

Establish what the client needs to receive and how the findings will be used.

05 — Confirm scope and timeframe

Structure the assignment around practical requirements, priorities and deadlines.
