PROJECT 05 • SOCIAL & LABOUR-MARKET RESEARCH

Youth Employment & Skills Development Analysis

A social research case study examining youth employment, skills development, employability and barriers to economic opportunity.

CASE STUDY OVERVIEW

Understanding the relationship between skills and opportunity.

Project 05 examines youth employment and skills development through an evidence-based social and labour-market research framework.

The analysis is designed to identify employment patterns, skills gaps, barriers and opportunities relevant to programmes and policy.


RESEARCH QUESTIONS

What the project investigates

  • What are the main employment outcomes among young people?
  • Which skills are associated with stronger employability?
  • Where are the major skills gaps?
  • What barriers prevent young people from accessing employment?
  • How do employment outcomes vary across groups?
  • What programme or policy interventions may improve outcomes?
