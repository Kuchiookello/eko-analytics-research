Youth Employment & Skills Development Analysis

An analytical study examining youth skills, employment outcomes, labour-market barriers and opportunities for strengthening employability interventions.

Explore Case Study Project Resources
PROJECT OVERVIEW

Understanding Skills and Employment Outcomes

Youth employment interventions require evidence about skills, labour-market participation, employment barriers and the relationship between training and economic opportunity.

This project demonstrates EKO's ability to analyse social and economic data and convert findings into programme and policy insights.

Project Profile

  • Category: Socio-economic research
  • Focus: Youth employment
  • Theme: Skills and employability
  • Application: Programme and policy decisions
  • Period: 2025–2026

The Challenge

Young people may complete training while continuing to experience barriers to employment. Organisations therefore need evidence that goes beyond participation numbers and examines actual employment and livelihood outcomes.

Objectives

  • Analyse youth employment status.
  • Assess skills and training participation.
  • Identify employment barriers.
  • Examine skills-to-market alignment.
  • Analyse livelihood outcomes.
  • Develop programme recommendations.
ANALYTICAL FRAMEWORK

Evidence Across the Employment Journey

Skills Analysis

Assessment of training participation, acquired competencies and skills-development patterns.

Employment Outcomes

Analysis of employment, self-employment, job-search and livelihood outcomes.

Programme Insights

Findings are translated into recommendations for improving employability interventions.

KEY QUESTIONS

What the Study Examines

Employment Indicators

  • Employment status
  • Training participation
  • Skills acquired
  • Job-search experience
  • Income and livelihood activity
  • Employment sustainability

Barriers & Opportunities

  • Skills mismatch
  • Limited work experience
  • Access to opportunities
  • Entrepreneurship constraints
  • Labour-market information gaps
  • Potential programme interventions
RECOMMENDATIONS

Supporting Better Youth Employment Decisions

Evidence from the analysis can support training providers, NGOs, development organisations and policymakers in designing interventions that better connect skills development with real employment and livelihood opportunities.
