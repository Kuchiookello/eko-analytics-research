PROJECT OVERVIEW

Understanding Markets Through Consumer Evidence

Successful market decisions depend on understanding why consumers purchase, what influences their choices and where unmet market needs exist.

This project demonstrates EKO's approach to transforming consumer and market information into commercially useful insights.

Project Profile

  • Category: Market Research
  • Focus: Consumer purchasing behaviour
  • Analysis: Market segmentation and opportunity assessment
  • Application: Product and market strategy
  • Period: 2025–2026

The Business Question

Businesses need reliable evidence about customer preferences, purchasing drivers, price sensitivity, competing alternatives and emerging market opportunities before committing resources.

Objectives

  • Analyse consumer purchasing patterns.
  • Identify major purchase drivers.
  • Examine customer preferences.
  • Identify meaningful market segments.
  • Assess competitive alternatives.
  • Identify potential market opportunities.
RESEARCH APPROACH

From Consumer Data to Market Intelligence

Market Research

Structured information is collected or organised around consumer needs, behaviours and preferences.

Segmentation

Consumers are examined across meaningful characteristics and behavioural patterns.

Opportunity Analysis

Evidence is translated into potential commercial and strategic opportunities.

ANALYSIS

Key Areas of Investigation

Consumer Behaviour

  • Purchase frequency
  • Product preferences
  • Price considerations
  • Brand influence
  • Purchase channels
  • Customer priorities

Market Opportunity

  • Unmet customer needs
  • Potential customer segments
  • Competitive positioning
  • Product/service opportunities
  • Channel opportunities
  • Market-entry considerations
DECISION SUPPORT

Turning Market Evidence into Strategy

The study is designed to support decisions relating to market positioning, customer targeting, product development, pricing, marketing communication and channel strategy.
