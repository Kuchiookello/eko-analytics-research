ABOUT EKO

Research with clarity. Analysis with purpose.

EKO Analytics & Research is an independent research, analytics and decision-support initiative focused on helping organisations turn information into clearer evidence, stronger insight and practical action.

WHO WE ARE

A practical research and analytics consultancy.

EKO Analytics & Research was created around a simple but important idea: organisations often have information, but not always the time, structure or analytical capacity to turn that information into useful decisions.

EKO brings together research, data analysis, interpretation, visualisation and professional communication to help clients move from scattered information to structured evidence, and from evidence to practical action.

The consultancy is designed to support organisations that need flexible, focused and professional analytical capacity without necessarily maintaining a large in-house research team.

OUR DIRECTION

Mission, vision and purpose.

Mission

To provide clear, structured and practical research and analytical support that helps organisations make better-informed decisions.

Vision

EKO APPROACH

Analytical work designed around decisions.

EKO does not begin with a chart or a report. It begins with the question the organisation needs answered.

Start with the Question Clarify the decision, problem or information gap before choosing the analytical method.
Structure the Evidence Organise available data, sources and information into a form that can be analysed reliably.
Analyse What Matters Focus analysis on relevant patterns, indicators, relationships, risks and opportunities.
Translate into Action Present findings in a way that helps decision-makers understand what should happen next.
CORE VALUES

Principles that guide EKO's work.

Integrity

Responsible handling of information, transparent methods and honest interpretation of findings.

Excellence

Careful research, strong analytical thinking, professional presentation and attention to detail.

Objectivity

Evidence before assumption, with clear separation between findings, interpretation and recommendation.

Practicality

Research and analytics should help organisations act, not simply produce technically impressive documents.

Collaboration

Working with clients to understand the real context, constraints and decisions behind the assignment.

Impact

Evidence should ultimately contribute to stronger organisations, better programmes and better decisions.

OUR METHODOLOGY

A structured path from information to action.

EKO uses a practical analytical workflow that can be adapted to business, research, programme and market assignments.

01 — Define

Clarify objectives, questions and decision needs.

02 — Gather

Identify relevant data, evidence and sources.

03 — Prepare

Clean, organise and validate information.

04 — Analyse

Examine patterns, trends, gaps and relationships.

05 — Communicate

Present evidence through clear reports, dashboards and visualisations.

06 — Recommend

Translate evidence into practical actions.
