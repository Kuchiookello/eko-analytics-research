CASE STUDY FRAMEWORK

A consistent professional analytical process.

While every project addresses a different question, EKO applies a consistent evidence-to-decision framework.

Problem Definition

Establish the business, programme, market or research question.

Evidence Preparation

Structure, clean and validate relevant information.

Analysis

Develop indicators, comparisons, trends and analytical measures.

Visualisation

Present important evidence through clear charts and dashboards.

Interpretation

Explain what the findings mean and why they matter.

Recommendations

Translate analytical evidence into practical next steps.

PORTFOLIO TRANSPARENCY

Demonstration work is clearly identified.

Where an assignment is an independent EKO portfolio project rather than commissioned client work, that distinction is clearly disclosed.

Synthetic or publicly available information may be used to demonstrate research methodology, analytical capability and decision-support thinking.

Confidential information from real engagements will not be published without appropriate permission.

EKO STANDARD TRANSPARENCY

Evidence should build trust.

Demonstration work is not presented as commissioned client work.

Methods, limitations and the nature of the underlying information are disclosed.
