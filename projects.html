PROJECT 01 ANALYTICAL SCOPE

What the SME analysis examines.

The project is designed around the questions a business owner or manager would need answered before making sales, customer and growth decisions.

Revenue Performance

Analysis of total revenue, monthly performance and changes in sales over time.

Transaction Analysis

Examination of transaction volumes, average transaction value and purchasing activity.

Customer Performance

Identification of customer segments, contribution patterns and high-value groups.

Product & Service Mix

Assessment of which products or services contribute most strongly to overall performance.

Geographic Performance

Comparison of revenue and activity across business locations or operating markets.

Management Priorities

Translation of analytical findings into practical priorities for management action.

PORTFOLIO TRANSPARENCY

Professional demonstration projects.

Where a project has not been commissioned by an external client, EKO clearly identifies it as an independent portfolio project.

Demonstration projects may use simulated or publicly available information to showcase analytical methodology, reporting capability and decision-support thinking without representing fictional organisations as real clients.

EKO STANDARD TRANSPARENCY

Evidence should build trust.

EKO does not present demonstration work as commissioned client work.

Client confidentiality will also be respected whenever real engagements are included in the portfolio.
