CASE STUDY FRAMEWORK

Every EKO project follows a consistent analytical structure.

This ensures that each case study demonstrates not only technical analysis but also clear reasoning, professional reporting and decision-support capability.

Problem Definition

Clarify the management, research, market or programme question.

Data Preparation

Structure, clean and validate the information needed for analysis.

Analytical Model

Develop relevant indicators, comparisons and analytical measures.

Dashboard

Present the most important findings through clear visualisations.

Interpretation

Explain what the analytical results mean and why they matter.

Recommendations

Translate evidence into practical management or programme actions.

PORTFOLIO TRANSPARENCY

Demonstration work is clearly identified.

Where a project has not been commissioned by an external client, EKO identifies it as an independent portfolio case study.

Demonstration projects may use synthetic or publicly available information to showcase research methodology, analytical capability, reporting and decision-support thinking without representing fictional organisations as real clients.

Client confidentiality will also be respected whenever commissioned assignments are included in the EKO portfolio.

EKO STANDARD TRANSPARENCY

Evidence should build trust.

Portfolio demonstration work is not presented as commissioned client work.

Methods, limitations and the nature of the information are clearly disclosed.
