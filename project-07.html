PROJECT 07 • BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE

SME Business Performance Dashboard

A management analytics case study integrating sales, customer, financial and operational indicators into a consolidated business-performance dashboard.

CASE STUDY OVERVIEW

A clearer management view of business performance.

Project 07 demonstrates how multiple areas of SME performance can be brought together into a concise management dashboard.

The project focuses on the indicators decision-makers need to monitor business performance, identify changes and prioritise management action.


BUSINESS QUESTIONS

What the dashboard helps answer

  • How is overall business performance changing?
  • Which KPIs require management attention?
  • How are sales and customer indicators performing?
  • What financial patterns are emerging?
  • Where are operational weaknesses or opportunities?
  • What information should management monitor regularly?
