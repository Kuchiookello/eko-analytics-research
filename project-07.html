PROJECT OVERVIEW

Making Business Performance Visible

SMEs often possess valuable sales, customer and operational information but lack a simple management system for monitoring the indicators that matter most.

This project demonstrates how EKO Analytics & Research can transform operational data into a practical business-performance dashboard.

Project Profile

  • Category: Business Analytics
  • Focus: SME performance
  • Output: Management dashboard
  • Application: Business decision support
  • Period: 2025–2026

The Challenge

Business owners may rely on fragmented spreadsheets or informal observations, making it difficult to identify trends, compare performance and respond quickly to emerging problems.

Objectives

  • Define relevant business KPIs.
  • Consolidate performance information.
  • Analyse sales and customer trends.
  • Monitor operational performance.
  • Create management visualisations.
  • Support faster business decisions.
DASHBOARD FRAMEWORK

From Business Records to Management Intelligence

Data Consolidation

Relevant sales, customer and operational information is structured into an analytical dataset.

KPI Analysis

Core indicators are calculated and monitored across meaningful business dimensions.

Dashboard Design

Management information is presented through clear and decision-focused visualisations.

PERFORMANCE AREAS

What Management Can Monitor

Commercial KPIs

  • Revenue performance
  • Sales trends
  • Customer activity
  • Average transaction value
  • Product/service performance
  • Customer retention

Management Insights

  • Performance against targets
  • Growth trends
  • High-performing areas
  • Underperformance
  • Emerging risks
  • Priority management actions
BUSINESS VALUE

Supporting Faster, Better-Informed Decisions

A well-designed performance dashboard gives management a concise view of business health and helps turn routine operational information into evidence for planning, performance management and growth.
